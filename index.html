How I work

The same five steps run through every case study above, whether the team is in a credit office, a cable factory or an LPG bottling plant.

Step 1

Discover

Ride along with the team and baseline the real numbers before proposing anything.

Step 2

Design

Agree the rules with the people who own them, such as credit policy, incentive slabs or plant SOP steps.

Step 3

Build

Do the heavy lifting with Python, GenAI and Power BI, and ship it in a format the team already trusts.

Step 4

Adopt

Pilot with a tight feedback loop, log overrides instead of blocking them, and track usage.

Step 5

Scale

Hand over with a runbook, a named owner and a review cadence, so it keeps working after I leave.
